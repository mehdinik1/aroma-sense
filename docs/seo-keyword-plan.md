## 4. Fix the existing blog before adding more

- **Only 24 of 140 posts link to a product or collection.** Add a relevant product link to the top 30 posts. This is the cheapest ranking and revenue win available.
- **Duplicates:** only one exact duplicate title exists ("Relaxing Activities to Do Before the End of Summer", 2 posts) and no bodies are copied. Keep one and redirect the other.
- **Off-topic drift:** posts on collagen, binge-watching, and fall resolutions dilute topical focus. Keep them out of the sitemap or noindex them if they get no traffic.
- **Author names:** "aromasenseusa Admin" and "Customer Service" look weak. Publish under a real named author.

## 5. Cautions

- **Health claims:** queries about eczema, acne, dandruff and hair loss are tempting, but claiming a shower filter treats these can breach advertising rules. Write "may help reduce dryness and irritation from chlorine", never "treats" or "cures". Cite sources.
- **Competitor names** (Voesh, Jolie and others appear in real queries): comparison posts naming them invite trademark and accuracy problems. Compare filter *types* instead.
- **Reviews:** "reviews" queries are high intent. Collect real customer reviews and add review markup only when they exist. Do not fabricate ratings.
